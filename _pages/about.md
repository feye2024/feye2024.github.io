# 👨🏻‍🎓 <font color="#4A708B">About Me</font>

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Industrial Engineering at Clemson University <img src="./images/clemson.png" style='width: 1.5em;'>. Before joining Clemson, I received my Ph.D. in Industrial and Systems Engineering in 2025 and my M.Sc. in Statistics in 2023 from Rutgers University–New Brunswick <img src="./images/rutgers.png" style='width: 1.5em;'>.

<!--My research aims to advance the integration of modern AI/ML, statistics, and physics by constructing physics-guided data science models for industrial applications such as energy and manufacturing.-->

My research focuses on AI/ML and statistical modeling for scientific and engineering systems. I develop methods that learn from data while incorporating domain knowledge and governing principles, with applications in weather, energy, and industrial applications.
